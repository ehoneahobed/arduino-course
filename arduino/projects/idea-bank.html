Why

How to pick one

A list of ideas is a menu, not a muse. Every idea below gets better the moment you find a real person who has that problem and ask them about it. So use the list like this.

  1. Start from the last band you finished, and read up the list from there. Everything at or below your band, you already have the parts and the ideas for.
  2. Pick the one where you can name the person. Your aunt with the chop bar beats a perfect idea for nobody.
  3. Go and ask them what goes wrong now. Write it down in their words. You will almost always change the rule after you hear them.
  4. Run it through the four questions on Invent a project: who is it for, is there really a rule in it, can you tell whether it worked, and does it fit in 18 pins and your actual box.
  5. Fill in the one-sheet plan from the same page before you touch a wire.

Every device on this page has the same shape you met on the Invent page: something it notices, a rule it applies, something it does. The rule is the part that makes it a device and not a switch. If you can change the rule and make it better for your person, do.

Stars, and what they mean

★☆☆ an afternoon, mostly one new idea.  ★★☆ an evening or two, with something to measure.  ★★★ a few evenings, several ideas at once, and a pin budget worth doing on paper first.

The rules that come with every idea

Nothing here touches mains electricity: never a socket, a plug, a mains cable, a pump, a bell or a lamp that plugs into the wall. Every device will lose power one day, so decide what it does when the power comes back. Keep boards and batteries dry, and keep fingers out of anything that moves. A device that helps with health, food or safety is a helper to a person who stays in charge, never a replacement for them.

The best one is not on this page

Walk round the hub, the compound or the market for an hour and ask three people what goes wrong in their day that nobody has fixed. That idea is worth more than all 52 of these, because somebody is waiting for it. When you have it, take it to Invent a project and fill in the plan.
