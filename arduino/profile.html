Your data

Move or clear your progress

Export saves your progress as a small file. Import loads that file on another computer (or after the browser was cleared). Clear removes your progress from this computer, which is the right thing to do on a shared computer. Your light or dark choice is kept.

Clear everything on this computer? Your sections read, answers, badges, 3D missions, edited sketches and name will all be removed. This cannot be undone, unless you exported a file first.
