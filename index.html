How

What every course here gives you

Learn alone, at your speed

Every step says what you should see, and what to do if you do not see it. There is no class to keep up with.

3D labs

Turn the board and every part in your hand, see inside the breadboard, and try ideas before you build them. See them all in the 3D Workshop.

Works offline

Copy the site to a USB stick and open it on any computer, with no internet at all.

Real projects

Every course ends with something a real person in Nzemaland asked for, tested on them, not only on you.

Free

Nothing to pay and nothing to sign up for. The Arduino Ladder's lessons are Creative Commons and its code is MIT.

For facilitators too

Running a group at the Hub or a school? Each course has a facilitator guide.
